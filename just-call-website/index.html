GOOD TO KNOW

A few simple answers.

Questions about Just Call? Here's what to expect.

What is Just Call?+

Just Call is a simple Android reminder app that helps you keep in touch with loved ones through phone calls.

Does Just Call make calls for me?+

No. It helps you remember to call and gives you an easy way to start the call yourself.

Can I choose how often I'm reminded?+

Yes. You can choose a different reminder frequency for each person you add.

Is this a messaging or social media app?+

No. Just Call is intentionally focused on call reminders—without social feeds or messaging distractions.
